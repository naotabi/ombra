How Ombra works

One sentence, then your turn

Ombra plays a sentence, repeats it, then waits.

  • You hear the sentence.
  • It repeats.
  • A pause opens up. Say it out loud.
  • The next sentence begins.
Text: Show → Text: Hide

Reading along is fine at first. But the point is to leave it behind.
